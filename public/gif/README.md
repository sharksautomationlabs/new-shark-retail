# Shark GIF – Transparent background chahiye

Hero me jo shark GIF lag rahi hai, uska **background remove** karne ke liye GIF ko **transparent background** wali banani padegi. Code se pixels change nahi ho sakte, isliye ye step follow karo:

## Steps (online, free)

1. **Ezgif (GIF + background remove)**  
   - Jaao: [https://ezgif.com/remove-background](https://ezgif.com/remove-background)  
   - "Choose file" se apni `sharks.gif` upload karo (ya jo bhi shark GIF hai).  
   - "Upload and remove background" pe click karo.  
   - Result download karo (transparent background wali GIF).

2. **Remove.bg (pehle frame/image ke liye)**  
   - Agar GIF nahi chal rahi: [https://www.remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg) pe ek frame upload karke background remove karo, phir kisi GIF maker se animated banao.  
   - Ya Ezgif use karo – wo GIF ke saath kaam karta hai.

3. **Replace file**  
   - Jo nayi transparent GIF download hui, uska naam rakh do: **`sharks.gif`**  
   - Isi folder (`public/gif/`) me purani `sharks.gif` ko replace kar do (backup le lo agar chahiye).

Uske baad site pe wahi particles background shark ke peeche dikhega, bina extra solid background ke.
